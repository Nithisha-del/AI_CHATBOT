TRAVEL CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can make travel bookings through supported travel services.

Customers should check the booking details carefully before confirming a reservation.

Customers can cancel eligible travel bookings according to the cancellation policy.

Refund amounts depend on the booking type, cancellation time, and service provider policy.

Travel booking changes may be subject to availability and additional charges.

Customers should keep their booking ID when contacting support.

Customers can contact support for booking changes, cancellations, refunds, payment issues, and booking-related problems.

Travel schedules can change because of weather, operational conditions, or service-provider decisions.

Customers should check the latest booking information before travelling.

Customers should not share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete payment-card details with customer support.
