HEALTHCARE CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact healthcare service support for appointment booking, appointment cancellation, hospital or clinic information, billing questions, and service-related issues.

Customers should provide their appointment ID or booking details when contacting support about a specific appointment.

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled according to the healthcare provider's policy.

Appointment availability depends on the selected doctor, clinic, hospital, date, and time.

Healthcare customer support can help with administrative and service-related questions.

The chatbot should not diagnose diseases or provide emergency medical treatment instructions.

For medical emergencies, customers should contact local emergency medical services or visit an appropriate emergency facility.

Customers should protect their medical records and personal information.

Customers should not share unnecessary sensitive information in a public chat.
