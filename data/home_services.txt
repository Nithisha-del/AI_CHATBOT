HOME SERVICES CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can book home services such as cleaning, appliance repair, plumbing, electrical services, and other supported services.

Customers should provide the booking ID and service address when contacting support about a specific booking.

Service availability depends on location, service type, and available professionals.

Customers can cancel or reschedule a booking according to the service provider's cancellation policy.

Additional charges may apply for certain services, materials, emergency visits, or work outside the original service request.

Customers can report incomplete, incorrect, or unsatisfactory services to customer support.

Customers should verify the service details and estimated charges before confirming a booking.

Customers should not share passwords, OTPs, or financial credentials with service providers.

Customers can contact support for booking, cancellation, rescheduling, payment, and service-quality issues.
