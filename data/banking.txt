BANKING CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact banking support for account-related service questions, transaction issues, card problems, and digital banking problems.

Customers should keep their transaction reference number when reporting a transaction issue.

If a transaction fails but money is debited, the customer should check the transaction status and contact the bank if the amount is not reversed within the expected time.

Customers should immediately report unauthorized transactions to their bank.

Customers should block a lost or stolen card through the bank's official channels.

Customers should never share their PIN, password, OTP, CVV, or complete card number with anyone claiming to provide support.

Banking services may include account information, card services, fund transfers, digital payments, and transaction support.

Processing time for refunds and reversals depends on the type of transaction and financial institution.

Customers should use only official banking applications, websites, phone numbers, or branches for sensitive banking requests.
