CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

GENERAL SUPPORT

Our customer support team helps customers with orders, payments, delivery, cancellations, returns, refunds, accounts, and product-related questions.

ORDERS

Customers can place orders through our website or mobile application. After placing an order, customers can check the order status from the Orders section of their account.

ORDER STATUS

Order statuses may include Order Placed, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.

ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers can request cancellation before the order is shipped. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be available.

DELIVERY

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days. Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location, product availability, weather, and other conditions.

TRACKING

Customers can track their order using the tracking option available in their account. A tracking number may be provided after the order is shipped.

RETURNS

Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery for eligible products. The product should generally be unused and in acceptable condition.

REFUNDS

Refunds are processed after the returned product is received and checked. The refund time may depend on the payment method and bank.

DAMAGED PRODUCTS

If a product arrives damaged, the customer should contact customer support as soon as possible and provide photographs or other required details.

WRONG PRODUCT

If the customer receives a product different from the ordered product, they should contact customer support to request a replacement or return.

PAYMENTS

Customers can pay using credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking.

PAYMENT FAILURE

If a payment fails but money is deducted from the customer's account, the customer should wait for the payment status to update. If the amount is not automatically returned, customer support can investigate the transaction.

UPI PAYMENTS

UPI payments may fail because of network problems, bank issues, incorrect payment details, or temporary service problems.

ACCOUNT

Customers can create an account using their basic personal information and contact details. Customers should keep their login credentials private.

PASSWORD

If a customer forgets their password, they can use the password reset option on the login page.

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Customers can check product information, availability, price, specifications, and other details on the product page.

OUT OF STOCK

If a product is out of stock, customers can check again later or look for alternative products.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact customer support for help with orders, payments, delivery, returns, refunds, accounts, and product issues.

PRIVACY

Customers should not share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or other confidential security information with anyone, including customer support representatives.

SECURITY

Customer support will never ask customers to share their password, OTP, PIN, or full payment credentials.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Question: How can I track my order?
Answer: Open your account and go to the Orders section to view the order status and tracking information.

Question: Can I cancel my order?
Answer: You can request cancellation before the order is shipped.

Question: How long does delivery take?
Answer: Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

Question: Can I return a product?
Answer: Eligible products can generally be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Question: When will I receive my refund?
Answer: A refund is processed after the returned product is received and checked. The exact time depends on the payment method.

Question: What should I do if I receive a damaged product?
Answer: Contact customer support as soon as possible and provide the required details and photographs.

Question: What payment methods are available?
Answer: Credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking are supported.

Question: I forgot my password. What should I do?
Answer: Use the password reset option on the login page.

Question: Is my account information safe?
Answer: Customers should keep their login credentials private and never share passwords, OTPs, or PINs.
