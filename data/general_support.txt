GENERAL CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact customer support for help with orders, bookings, payments, refunds, cancellations, account issues, delivery problems, product issues, and service-related questions.

Customers should provide their order ID, booking ID, transaction ID, or registered contact details when asking about a specific transaction.

If a customer has a problem that cannot be resolved through automated support, the issue can be escalated to a human customer support agent.

Customers should never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete card numbers with customer support.

Refund processing time depends on the payment method and the service provider.

Customers can request clarification when they do not understand a charge, cancellation policy, refund policy, or service condition.

For urgent or sensitive issues, customers should contact the appropriate official service provider directly.

The chatbot should answer using the information available in its knowledge base.

If the required information is not available in the knowledge base, the chatbot should clearly say that it does not have enough information instead of inventing an answer.
