TELECOM CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact telecom support for mobile recharge, network, SIM, data, calling, messaging, and account-related issues.

If a recharge fails but money is deducted, the amount is normally reversed according to the payment provider's processing time.

Customers should keep the recharge transaction ID when reporting a recharge problem.

Customers can contact support if their recharge is successful but the expected service or balance is not updated.

Network quality can vary depending on location, coverage, network traffic, weather, and technical conditions.

Customers can contact support for SIM-related problems and service activation issues.

Customers should not share OTPs, passwords, or PINs with unknown callers.

Customers should use official telecom support channels for account and SIM-related requests.

Customers can contact support for recharge problems, network issues, SIM issues, data problems, and service complaints.
