TECHNOLOGY CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact technology support for product information, account problems, software issues, device problems, subscriptions, and technical assistance.

Customers should provide the product name, device model, software version, or order information when relevant.

Customers should restart or update a device or application when instructed by official support.

Customers should install software only from trusted and official sources.

Customers should keep their software and operating system updated when updates are available.

Customers should contact support if a product is defective or if a software service is not working correctly.

Warranty and replacement eligibility depends on the product and manufacturer policy.

Customers should not share passwords, recovery codes, OTPs, or other security credentials with support agents.

Customers should back up important data before performing major device resets or software changes.
