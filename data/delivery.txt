DELIVERY CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Standard product deliveries normally take 3 to 5 business days.

Delivery time can vary depending on the customer's location, product availability, seller, courier service, traffic, weather, and other conditions.

Customers can check their order status using the order tracking option.

Customers should provide their order ID when asking about a specific delivery.

If an order is delayed, the customer can contact customer support.

If a package is marked delivered but has not been received, the customer should report the issue to customer support.

Customers should verify the delivery address before placing an order.

A customer may be contacted by the delivery service when the package is ready for delivery.

Customers should not share OTPs or sensitive account information with unknown callers or delivery personnel.

Customers can contact support for delayed deliveries, tracking problems, incorrect delivery addresses, missing packages, and delivery complaints.
