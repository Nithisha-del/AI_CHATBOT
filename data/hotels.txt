HOTEL CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can search for and book hotels through supported hotel services.

Customers should check room type, price, availability, facilities, check-in time, check-out time, and cancellation policy before confirming a booking.

Hotel bookings can be cancelled according to the cancellation policy associated with the booking.

Refund eligibility depends on the hotel's cancellation policy and the time of cancellation.

Customers should keep their hotel booking ID when contacting support.

Customers can contact support for booking confirmation, cancellation, refund, room-related issues, and payment problems.

Check-in and check-out times depend on the hotel.

Early check-in or late check-out may depend on hotel availability and may involve additional charges.

Customers should carry valid identification when required by the hotel.

Customers should not share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete card numbers with hotel support.
