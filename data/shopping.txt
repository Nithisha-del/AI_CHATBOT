SHOPPING CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can purchase products through the shopping service.

Customers can check product details, availability, price, and delivery information before placing an order.

Eligible products can usually be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Products returned should generally be unused and in their original condition and packaging.

Some products may not be eligible for return or exchange depending on the product category and seller policy.

Customers can request an exchange for eligible products if the received product is damaged, defective, incorrect, or has another valid issue.

Customers should provide the order ID when contacting support about a product.

If a customer receives the wrong product, the customer should report the issue to customer support.

If a product arrives damaged, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible.

Refunds are normally processed after the returned product is received and verified.

Refund processing can take 5 to 7 business days depending on the payment method.

Customers can contact support for questions about returns, exchanges, refunds, damaged products, incorrect products, and delivery.

Customers should not share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete card numbers with customer support.
