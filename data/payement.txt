PAYMENT CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can make payments using supported payment methods such as UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and other digital payment methods.

Cash on delivery may be available for selected orders and locations.

If a payment fails but the customer's account is debited, the amount is normally returned automatically within 3 to 5 business days.

Customers should contact support if a failed payment has not been refunded after the expected period.

Customers should keep the transaction ID when reporting a payment problem.

A payment can be marked successful only after confirmation is received from the payment system.

Customers should not make multiple payments for the same order if the first payment is still being processed.

If a customer is charged twice for the same transaction, the customer should provide the transaction details to customer support.

Refund time depends on the payment method and financial institution.

Customers should never share their OTP, PIN, password, CVV, or complete card number with customer support.

Customers can contact support for failed payments, duplicate payments, missing refunds, payment status, and transaction-related problems.
