FOOD CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Food orders can be placed through the food ordering service.

Customers can check the estimated delivery time before placing an order.

Food orders can usually be cancelled before the restaurant starts preparing the order.

Once food preparation has started, cancellation may not be possible.

Food orders are normally delivered within 30 to 45 minutes, but delivery time can vary depending on location, traffic, restaurant workload, and weather.

Customers can contact support if their food order is delayed.

If the delivered food is missing, incorrect, damaged, or different from the ordered items, the customer can report the issue to customer support.

Customers may request a refund for missing, incorrect, or damaged food after the issue is verified.

Refund processing time depends on the payment method.

Customers should provide their order ID when reporting a food-order problem.

Customers can contact support for questions about food orders, delivery, cancellation, refunds, and complaints.

Food orders may support payment methods such as UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and cash on delivery when available.

Customers should not share their passwords, OTPs, PINs, or complete card numbers with customer support.
